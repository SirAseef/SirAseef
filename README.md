## 👨‍💼 About Me

I lead academics and teach at **Aptech**, where I serve as **Senior Manager Academics** and **Senior Faculty**. Over the years I have taught across Aptech's full range of curricula and courses — from programming fundamentals and web development to mobile, databases, data science and AI — and I mentor students through eProjects and competitions such as **Techwiz**.

I hold an **MS in Data Science from NED University**, where my research focused on **scalable Big Data and deep learning systems for climate-hazard forecasting** in Pakistan.

- 🎯 **Leadership:** academic planning, curriculum delivery, faculty coordination and quality assurance
- 🧑‍🏫 **Teaching:** every Aptech track — programming, web, mobile (Flutter & Dart), databases, data science & AI
- 🔬 **Research:** spatio-temporal deep learning, streaming data pipelines, climate AI
- 🤝 **Mentoring:** eProjects, Techwiz, final-year and industry-ready student projects
